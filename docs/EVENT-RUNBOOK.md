# Event runbook: The Fortune Teahouse (stub)

Draft stub, to be filled in during Phase 4 (see `PLAN.md`). Each section starts from what
`DESIGN.md` already says.

## Before the event

- Cookie order placed with enough lead time, after all 18 fortunes pass CI and a human read-through.
- Organizer sign-off on cookies at TeaGL, codes around the venue and the big screen.
- Game address announced early so phones load it before TeaGL; QR codes on tables.

## Cookie handling

- **Mix both packs together before handing out.** Each pack holds only 3 of each machine's 6 clues, so one pack alone can never finish a machine.
- **Hold back about 10 at random** for the lost fortunes jar, never stacks of one fortune.
- **Allergen card:** print the vendor's written allergen statement and keep it at the table (wheat and egg at least).

## TeaGL kickoff script

- TeaGL is 30 to 40 minutes at about 3:30pm. Cookies out, explain the story in a minute, point to the address.
- Tell people to find crewmates by their fortunes; no cookie is fine, pick a crew in the game.
- Say lucky words aloud freely: finding people is the only gate.

## During day one

- Machines are fixed in short bursts between talks; finished crews become maintainers and send hints.
- Watch the big screen for stalled crews and nudge them.
- Check the scoreboard is up; the game still plays without it.

## Finale opening

- Open when all three machines run, or at the end of day one as a safety net.
- Handoffs that touch an unfinished machine wait; the others go ahead.

## Closing session

- Run the full line end to end on the big screen; the first cookie out holds the gnu's last fortune.
- Show the completed README and the contributors list.

## Safety nets

- Any 4 of a machine's 6 clues is enough, and the four approachable styles always reach that.
- The whole game works without a cookie, and without the scoreboard (only shared progress pauses).
- The lost fortunes jar covers latecomers; any k of the N gnu's notes are enough.
